03 / meet marlowe

MEET MARLOWE

She's me.
And she isn't.

Marlowe Rose is what happened when I stopped keeping everything in my head.

I never set out to invent another person. Marlowe is still me—the stories are mine, the love is mine, the grief is mine, and so are all the messy little pieces in between.

I started making music because I realized I had things I wanted to say that ordinary words weren't quite holding anymore. Things about a marriage that's grown with me. About the life I thought I'd have and the life I ended up loving. About changing, getting older, taking up space, being afraid, laughing at myself, and doing things anyway.

Somewhere along the way, those stories became songs.

Marlowe is the name I gave that part of myself that decided to say it out loud.

I'm Megan. This is Marlowe.
