I’ve always had a loud mind and quiet words.

I’m shy. I take a while to open up. Growing up, even within my small group of friends, I often felt a little separate. They came to me for advice, but I didn’t always know how to share what was going on inside me.

I still think through so much before I speak. How will this make someone feel? How will it make me look? Is it right? Is it wrong? Sometimes, by the time I’ve questioned every word, I’ve kept them all to myself.

But being quiet has never meant there wasn’t much happening inside.

I’ve always wanted to do something meaningful. Something of my own that I could put my heart into. I’ve felt adrift for years, drawn to so many things but struggling to find something that felt like me.

Putting my thoughts and feelings into songs seemed like the natural progression after having an online diary full of atrocious poetry as a teenager. Apparently, I’m still trying to find creative ways to get out of my own head.

That diary gave me somewhere to be myself. Through music, I’m finding that freedom again—letting more of what I’ve kept inside become something I can share.

Maybe there’s someone else with a loud mind and quiet words who needs to hear something they haven’t been able to say.

That’s why I created Marlowe.

She is my voice out loud.

— Megan 🌹
