I joined a gym.

That's it. That's where this whole thing started.

I wasn't setting out to reinvent myself. I certainly wasn't planning on becoming someone who gets excited about tattoos, adds another piercing, or decides that putting her personal stories into songs sounds like a perfectly reasonable thing to do.

I just wanted to make a change.

And apparently, once I got started, I had a little trouble stopping.

The funny thing is, none of it happened all at once.

There wasn't some dramatic transformation where I suddenly woke up confident and ready to do everything I'd spent years avoiding.

It was more like one thing led to another.

I'd try something, realize it wasn't nearly as impossible as I'd convinced myself it would be, and then start wondering what else I'd been missing out on.

And somewhere along the way, I started surprising myself.

I found myself wearing things I wouldn't have been comfortable wearing before. Dresses. Shorts. Clothes that I actually liked instead of clothes I chose because I thought they were supposed to hide something.

Then there were the tattoos.

And the piercings.

Let's just say the woman who once worried so much about what other people might think has developed a pretty impressive collection of things for people to have opinions about.

And I love them.

Not because I'm trying to prove anything to anybody. But because I'm finally allowing myself to enjoy things simply because I want to.

That might sound like a small thing, but for me, it really wasn't.

I spent a lot of years being careful. Staying comfortable. Avoiding attention. Convincing myself that certain things just weren't for someone like me.

And I'm still not the most adventurous or confident person in the room.

But I've started asking myself a different question.

Instead of automatically thinking, *I could never do that*, I find myself wondering, *Well, why not?*

Which brings me to probably the most unexpected part of this whole journey.

Music.

Somehow, the woman who spent years writing down her feelings privately is now turning those feelings into songs and sharing them with the world.

I still have moments when I wonder what on earth I'm doing.

Actually, I have those moments pretty regularly.

But I also know how much I'd have missed if I'd kept talking myself out of everything.

I thought I was making one change in my life.

I had no idea how many other doors it might open.

And I have a feeling I'm not finished surprising myself yet.
